# Founder Financial Planning Session — Product Research

## Context

The idea for Family Financial AI emerged during a detailed personal financial planning conversation.

The conversation was initially intended only to produce a ten-year household financial plan.

During the process, several product insights appeared organically.

## Observation 1: Financial truth changed as details were clarified

Examples included:

- recurring loan payments were already included in a household-expense figure
- a final vehicle payment was temporary rather than recurring
- future monthly income was a planning average rather than guaranteed monthly net income
- business equity existed but should not be counted as liquid wealth

### Insight

A chat transcript cannot safely function as canonical financial state.

The system needs explicit, correctable structured data.

---

## Observation 2: The household needed rules, not only budgets

Useful rules included:

- eliminate high-cost revolving credit-card debt
- pay credit-card statement balances in full
- preserve low-cost loans instead of automatically prepaying
- build emergency liquidity before aggressive mortgage prepayment
- route income increases to wealth before lifestyle
- pre-fund travel
- use a discretionary spending allowance instead of tracking every small purchase
- unlock luxury purchases using wealth thresholds

### Insight

Financial Rule should be a first-class product concept.

---

## Observation 3: Long-term planning is mostly about tradeoffs

The user was less interested in:

> Can I technically pay for this?

and more interested in:

> If I do this, what happens to everything else?

### Insight

Scenario modeling may be one of the most important product capabilities.

---

## Observation 4: Users want long-term institutional memory

A key future question emerged:

> How am I doing compared with the plan we made when I started these new jobs?

### Insight

The system needs historical snapshots, plan versions, and decision history.

---

## Observation 5: Financial planning is collaborative and behavioral

The household wants:

- enjoyment
- travel
- discretionary spending
- future luxury purchases

The goal is not austerity.

### Insight

The product should help the couple set high-level boundaries rather than micromanage one another.

---

## Observation 6: AI should sit above deterministic systems

A useful future experience:

> I just got a raise.

The AI should:

1. interpret the event
2. retrieve current household state
3. use deterministic calculations
4. identify relevant household rules/goals
5. propose an updated allocation
6. show impact
7. ask for approval before changing canonical state

### Insight

The AI is an orchestration and reasoning layer, not the financial database and not the calculator.

---

## Founder hypothesis

A genuinely useful personal financial AI is not primarily a chatbot.

It is:

**Structured financial state + planning engine + scenario engine + decision history + AI interface.**
