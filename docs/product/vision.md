# Product Vision

## Working name

Family Financial AI

## Vision

Create a persistent household financial intelligence system that understands where a family is, where it wants to go, what it has decided, what has changed, and what current choices mean for future goals.

The product should feel less like a budgeting app and less like a generic financial chatbot.

It should behave like a financial operating system for a household.

## Core user question

> Given where we are, where we're trying to go, what we've already decided, and what just changed — what should we do now?

## Why this product exists

Traditional personal-finance tools typically focus on:

- expense tracking
- static budgets
- account aggregation
- isolated calculators
- investment dashboards

Generic AI assistants can discuss finances conversationally, but long-running planning is fragile when important state exists only in chat history, summaries, or model memory.

This product should combine structured financial state with conversational reasoning.

## Long-term experience

A household should be able to ask:

- Can we afford this trip without delaying our mortgage goal?
- What happens if one income disappears for six months?
- If salary increases by 20%, where should the extra money go?
- Are we ahead or behind the plan we made five years ago?
- Can we afford a luxury car without weakening education or retirement goals?
- Should we accelerate the mortgage or invest more?
- What changed financially this month?
- What assumptions in our plan are now outdated?

The AI should answer using current structured household data and deterministic scenario tools.

## Initial scope

The first version is tailored specifically to Ralph and his wife.

This is deliberate.

The product should solve one household's real planning needs extremely well before any attempt is made to generalize it into a broader consumer product.

## Long-term outcome

The application becomes the canonical record of:

- household financial facts
- assumptions
- goals
- decisions
- financial rules
- historical snapshots
- scenario analyses
- recommendations and their rationale

Conversation becomes an interface to this state, not a replacement for it.
