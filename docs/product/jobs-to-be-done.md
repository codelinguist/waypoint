# Jobs to Be Done

## Core job

When our financial situation changes or we are considering a significant decision, help us understand what it means for the future and what our best next move is.

## Supporting jobs

### Understand current position

- Show our current assets, liabilities, cash flow, and net worth.
- Explain what changed since the previous month/year.
- Show which numbers are facts and which are assumptions.

### Make decisions

- Model large purchases.
- Compare paying debt versus investing.
- Model job loss or income reduction.
- Model salary increases.
- Model business capital deployment.
- Model travel or education spending.
- Compare several scenarios side by side.

### Stay on track

- Track emergency-fund milestones.
- Track mortgage payoff trajectory.
- Track retirement progress.
- Track children's education funding.
- Track business capitalization.
- Show plan versus actual.

### Preserve institutional memory

- Remember why a financial decision was made.
- Preserve assumptions that were active at the time.
- Surface outdated assumptions.
- Maintain an auditable timeline of major decisions.

### Reduce anxiety around uncertainty

- Turn an uncertain event into a quantified runway/scenario.
- Show which goals are still safe and which need adjustment.
- Avoid presenting false precision.
