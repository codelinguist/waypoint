# User Zero: Ralph and Family

## Household

Initial users:

- Ralph
- Ralph's wife
- Three children

The product should support collaborative household planning while keeping the interaction simple enough that it does not become another bookkeeping burden.

## Long-term family goals

By approximately age 45 / around 2036:

- mortgage fully paid
- retirement on a strong funded trajectory
- three children's university education substantially secured
- ability to experience a layoff without severe financial stress
- multiple businesses contributing to wealth
- international travel without destabilizing finances
- ability to own a luxury car without weakening core goals
- one highly paid job should be enough
- work should increasingly be compensated for expertise/value rather than simply hours
- more time should be available for learning or pursuing another talent

## Behavioral realities

The system must account for real household behavior rather than assuming perfect discipline.

Observed friction areas include:

- small online purchases accumulating unnoticed
- food delivery and fast-food spending
- travel spending increasing due to access to goods unavailable locally
- temptation to upgrade lifestyle when income rises
- desire to enjoy money without turning the financial plan into austerity

The preferred solution is not micromanagement.

The preferred system uses:

- top-level allocation rules
- separate spending buckets
- guilt-free discretionary spending
- travel sinking funds
- explicit thresholds for major luxury purchases
- automatic wealth allocation before money feels available to spend

## User preference

Ralph prefers systems thinking, clear rules, measurable outcomes, and practical tradeoff explanations over generic financial advice.
